From complaint box to decision system

Severity analysis

An OpenCV pipeline measures the dark cavity area against the surrounding road surface and the crack density, giving a 0-100 score. If an Anthropic API key is configured, a Claude vision model also rates the photo and the two scores are blended. The app always works offline using OpenCV alone.

Rain-linked risk model

Rainfall for the past 3 days and the next 2 days comes from Open-Meteo (cached, with an offline fallback). Wetter weather raises the chance that weak spots worsen. Risk also grows with severity, repeat reports and how long a defect has been open.

Priority ranking

Priority = 0.40 severity + 0.35 risk + 0.15 reports + 0.10 age. It is transparent on purpose, so authorities can see why a road ranks where it does. Scores of 55+ mean fix now, 35-54 soon, below that monitor.

Smart duplicates

Reports within 25 metres of an open pothole merge into it and raise its report count, so the queue stays clean while repeated complaints still count as a signal.

Limits

The OpenCV score is a heuristic, not a trained model, and works best on close, daylight photos. It supports human judgement and does not replace inspection.
